HTML autoplay and loop example

The <video> element needs a direct MP4 file, such as sample.mp4. It cannot use a YouTube watch link as its source. Add a local MP4 you own or have permission to use beside this HTML file:

What the attributes mean

  • autoplay asks the browser to start the local video automatically.
  • muted starts it without sound; browsers commonly require this for autoplay.
  • loop restarts the local video when it reaches the end.
  • controls displays play, pause, seek, and volume controls.
<video controls autoplay muted loop>
  <source src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/vv6Cn16scjU?playlist=vv6Cn16scjU&loop=1">>
</video>

Autoplay may be blocked by browser settings. The YouTube player above remains viewer-controlled; the loop example uses your local MP4 file.
