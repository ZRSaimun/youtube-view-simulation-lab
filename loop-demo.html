What each attribute means

autoplay

Asks the browser to start playback when the video is ready. Browser policies commonly require muted playback for autoplay.

muted

Starts without sound. This improves the chance that browser autoplay is allowed; the viewer can unmute with the native controls.

loop

Restarts the video automatically when it reaches the end.

controls

Shows the browser’s built-in playback controls, including play, pause, seek, volume, and fullscreen where supported.

W3Schools-style HTML example

<video width="640" controls autoplay muted loop>
  <source src=src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/vv6Cn16scjU?autoplay=1&mute=1">>
  Your browser does not support the video tag.
</video>

Local-file note: This page only loads a video you add to the project or choose from your device. It does not embed or automatically play a third-party video.
